Mandatory North Carolina Election Law

Two Critical Rules for Voting on Election Day:

1. You CANNOT vote at any polling site — You MUST go to your specifically assigned precinct.

During early voting, voters could visit any site in the county. On Election Day, your ballot is printed specifically for your home precinct. If you go to the wrong precinct, you will have to vote a provisional ballot, and races outside that precinct may not be counted.

2. Same-Day Voter Registration is NOT available on Election Day.

Under NC General Statutes (§ 163-82.6), same-day registration is permitted only during Early Voting. On Election Day, you must already be registered to vote in North Carolina.

In Line by 7:30 PM? STAY IN LINE! Under North Carolina law, if you are physically in line at your assigned polling place by 7:30 PM on Election Day, election officials are legally required to keep the polls open until you have cast your ballot. Do not leave the line!

Warren Wilson College Student ID is Valid on Election Day!

The North Carolina State Board of Elections officially approved the Physical WWC Student ID for voter photo identification. You can use your physical plastic student ID to vote.

⚠️ CRITICAL: PHYSICAL CARD REQUIRED. Digital IDs stored on mobile phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, photos) are NOT ACCEPTED under NC law.

✅ Other Accepted Photo IDs: NC Driver’s License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID, or free county BOE ID card.

💡 Lacking an ID at the polls? Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at your precinct and cast a ballot that counts.

📋 Election Day Checklist

  • 1. Verify Assigned Precinct: Double-check your polling place at vt.ncsbe.gov/pplkup before you leave.
  • 2. Physical Photo ID: Bring your physical plastic ID card (phone images are not permitted).
  • 3. Voting Hours: 6:30 AM to 7:30 PM. (If in line by 7:30 PM, stay in line!).
  • 4. Notes Allowed: You can bring a paper voter guide or handwritten notes into the booth.
