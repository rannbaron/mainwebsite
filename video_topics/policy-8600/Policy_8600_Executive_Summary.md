## 1. Overview & Policy Objective
During the May 28, 2026 Durham Public Schools Board of Education Monthly Meeting, the Board conducted a comprehensive first reading and deliberation on **Policy 8600: Budget Transparency**. 

The policy establishes formal district guidelines for public reporting, routine budget updates, financial indicator tracking, and increased board and community oversight over district allocations and operational funds.

## 3. Formal Motions & Votes

- **Motion to Approve on First Read:**  
  *Moved by Board Member Emily Chávez:* Motion to approve Policy 8600 (Budget Transparency) on first reading, with an incorporated amendment to Section 4A specifying that reporting on outlined indicators shall occur within a **30-day window**.
- **Second:**  
  *Seconded by Board Member (now Chair) Joy Harrell-Goff.*
- **Board Vote & Outcome:**  
  The motion carried unanimously on first reading (presided over by then-Chair Bettina Umstead).
- **Next Steps:**  
  The amended policy language is scheduled to return for its second reading at the upcoming **June 9, 2026 Work Session**.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways
1. **Public Dashboards & Transparency:** Mandates routine, intelligible reporting on budgetary variances and staffing expenditures.
2. **30-Day Reporting Mandate:** Ensures staff reports financial indicator data within 30 days of period close.
3. **Timeline Alignment:** Coordinated with upcoming FY 2026–2027 fiscal year preparations and the June 9 work session.
