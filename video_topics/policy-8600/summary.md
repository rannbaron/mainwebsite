## Sources of Disagreement & Debate Alignment
### 1. Reporting Deadlines & Finance Staff Compliance Burden vs. Public Transparency
Deliberation over the timeline for publishing quarterly budget transparency reports. Board members debated whether requiring publication strictly 'within 30 days' placed an unrealistic operational burden on the finance department during period reconciliations, versus the public interest in ensuring timely and unambiguous financial reporting.

**Side A: Advocating Flexibility & Acknowledging Staff Capacity**
- Concerns regarding the finance department's capacity to audit and finalize quarterly indicators within tight windows
- Avoiding policies that setting staff up for compliance defaults during heavy budget seasons
*Members / Alignment:* Board discussion highlighting staff capacity considerations

**Side B: Advocating Strict Accountability & Clarified 30-Day Window**
- Ambiguity of '30 days after' vs 'within 30 days'
- Ensuring community dashboards and school board oversight are not delayed by open-ended timelines
*Members / Alignment:* Natalie Beyer, Emily Chávez

**Resolution:** Amended by consensus on first reading: Emily Chávez moved the policy with an explicit amendment to Section 4A specifying that reporting occur 'within 30 days'. The revised text was approved unanimously to return for second reading on June 9.
