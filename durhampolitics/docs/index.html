Durham Politics Tracker Documentation

Complete reference for navigating meeting archives, voting alignments, executive summary PDFs, real-time live meeting monitoring, and user account access across Durham's three major local governing bodies.

1. Scope & Coverage

The Durham Politics Tracker automatically ingests, transcribes, and analyzes public meetings from Durham's three primary elected local boards from each newest cohort's seating date forward:

2. Dashboard Navigation

  • Overview & Macro Trends: High-level KPI cards (Tracked Meetings, Recorded Formal Votes, Contested Roll Calls, Deliberation Issues), cross-body fault line summaries, and recent landmark votes.
  • Board Tabs (DPS School Board, City Council, County Commissioners): Displays all currently seated members' vote totals, Aye rate, Dissent rate, full voting history modal (Inspect Full Voting Record), and every tracked meeting in reverse chronological order.
  • Member Directory: Filter officials across all bodies by Current Serving Officials, Preceding / Former Members, or All.
  • Voting Alignments & Heatmaps: Board-specific pairwise voting agreement grids and Dissent Frequency Leaderboards.
  • Meeting Archives & Video: Master filterable timeline across all three boards by body, year, and global search keywords.

3. Consent Agenda Filter

By default, blanket Consent Agenda and Settle Agenda votes are excluded from all vote counts, member totals, dissent percentages, pairwise alignment grids, and meeting vote lists.

Pulled Items Always Count: Any item pulled off the consent agenda for separate discussion or a standalone vote is always included in the counts by default.

To include blanket consent agenda votes back into all calculations and lists, check the Include Consent Agenda checkbox in the top header bar. Every KPI, member card, alignment table, and vote list updates immediately.

4. Pairwise Heatmaps & Dissent Leaderboard

Upper-Triangular Pairwise Agreement Grid

Select a board from the dropdown to inspect pairwise agreement among its currently seated members. Because agreement is symmetric, only the main diagonal and upper triangle are displayed to avoid duplicate cells:

  • ≥ 95% — High voting agreement (green)
  • 90% – 94.9% — Moderate divergence (yellow)
  • < 90% — Frequent disagreement (red)

Dissent Frequency Leaderboard

The leaderboard directly beneath the grid is strictly scoped to the currently selected board. Each member's Dissent Rate measures NAY votes cast against carried majority motions divided by total recorded votes cast (dissents / votes_cast).

5. Video Timestamp Links & Executive Summary PDFs

Every meeting card provides direct verification back to the primary video record:

  • Full Meeting Video: Click the meeting title or the Watch Video Recording button to open the official YouTube recording.
  • Agenda Topics & Timestamps: Expand Agenda Topics & Timestamps on any meeting card to view chronological agenda items, each with a clickable ► HH:MM:SS deep link (&t=<seconds>s) jumping directly to that topic in the video.
  • Roll Call Votes & Debates: Every individual vote (including inside each official's Inspect Full Voting Record modal) and debate card links directly to its exact timestamp in the video, and includes a detailed Discussion summary.
  • Executive Summary PDF: Click Executive Summary PDF on any meeting to open a downloadable, print-ready report (/durhampolitics/summaries/<meeting_id>.pdf) containing meeting metadata, executive overview, chronological agenda discussions, detailed per-vote Discussion & Deliberation summaries, formal vote breakdowns, policy debates, and clickable timestamp hyperlinks embedded inside the PDF.

6. Live Meeting in Progress

When any of the three tracked Durham boards is actively broadcasting live on YouTube, a pulsing red Live Meeting in Progress tab appears automatically in the navigation bar.

Clicking the tab opens /durhampolitics/live.html in a split-pane layout:

  • Left Pane: Dynamically updating Executive Summary of Discussion So Far (overview, agenda topics covered so far, and motions/votes observed), refreshed every 60 seconds.
  • Top Right Pane: Embedded live video player of the ongoing broadcast.
  • Bottom Right Pane: Auto-scrolling running transcript generated in real time from the video's audio stream (with search filter and auto-scroll toggle).

7. Audio ASR Processing & GitHub Automation

All ongoing monitoring and archival run automatically via GitHub Actions (durham_live_and_archive.yml) every 5 minutes:

  • Live Real-Time Audio ASR (No Captions): During live meetings, audio is streamed directly from the video via yt-dlp + ffmpeg and transcribed in rolling 20-second chunks using faster-whisper, normalized with Durham official name registries, and summarized every 60 seconds.
  • Dual Audio + Captions Archival: When a meeting concludes, the pipeline runs both audio ASR and caption extraction (dual_transcriber.py), scores both transcripts for reliability, picks the more reliable source, updates durham_politics.db, generates the Executive Summary PDF, and redeploys the site automatically.

8. Accounts, Approval & Privacy

  • Requesting Access: New users can sign up at /durhampolitics/signup. Passwords are salted and hashed (SHA-256) in the browser before submission; plaintext passwords are never emailed or stored.
  • Administrator Approval: Every signup triggers an immediate approval notification to rann@rannbar-on.com, which can be approved or revoked via the GitHub Actions workflow.
  • Strict Page Isolation: Approved user accounts grant access exclusively to https://rannbar-on.com/durhampolitics/ (including its live view, PDFs, and docs) and are denied access to all other private pages on rannbar-on.com.
  • Idle Session Timeout: Authenticated sessions remain active across page navigation and automatically time out after 1 hour of idle time.
