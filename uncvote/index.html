⚠️
Mandatory NC State Law Requirement

You MUST Bring a PHYSICAL Photo ID (No Phone/Digital Cards!)

CRITICAL COURT RULING: The NC Court of Appeals issued an injunction prohibiting the use of mobile/digital UNC One Cards for voting. You MUST bring a PHYSICAL, plastic photo ID (physical UNC One Card, NC driver's license, or US passport). If you only have a mobile card, visit the UNC One Card Office in the Student Union to obtain a physical card.

🚫 Mobile digital IDs on your phone are NOT ACCEPTED at polling sites under North Carolina law.

✅ ACCEPTED: Physical UNC One Card, NC Driver's License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID.

💡 Need an ID on the spot? You can still vote! Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at the polling place or obtain a free voter photo ID from the Orange County Board of Elections.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Orange County, or if you moved to a new local address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Physical UNC One Card (physical card).
  • ✓ On-campus UNC students can use their physical UNC One Card and campus housing proof (viewable on the ConnectCarolina portal or housing assignment letter).
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Approved Photo ID: Physical UNC One Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Document showing your name and current local Orange County address (university official portal/letter, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or lease; can be electronic on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Physical UNC One Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport. Double-check you have the plastic card, not your phone!
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Physical campus ID (on campus) or local address document (off campus).
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on Orange County Board of Elections to preview candidates before you vote.
  • 4. You Can Bring Notes: You are legally permitted to bring a written list or notes into the voting booth (just do not take photos while voting).
