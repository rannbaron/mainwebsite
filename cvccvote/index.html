Student Election Day Voting Guide

Early voting has concluded. On Election Day (Tuesday, November 3), North Carolina law requires you to vote at your specifically assigned precinct polling place. Find your precinct, bring your photo ID, and cast your ballot before 7:30 PM.

[NOTICE]
Mandatory North Carolina Election Law

Two Critical Rules for Voting on Election Day:

1. You CANNOT vote at any polling site — You MUST go to your specifically assigned precinct.

During early voting, voters could visit any site in the county. On Election Day, your ballot is printed specifically for your home precinct. If you go to the wrong precinct, you will have to vote a provisional ballot, and races outside that precinct may not be counted.

2. Same-Day Voter Registration is NOT available on Election Day.

Under NC General Statutes (§ 163-82.6), same-day registration is permitted only during Early Voting. On Election Day, you must already be registered to vote in North Carolina.

[REQUIRED]
Alternative Photo ID Required

Student ID Card is NOT Accepted for Voting

Under North Carolina law, Catawba Valley Community College student IDs are not approved for voting. You must present an approved alternative photo ID.

Acceptable Photo IDs on Election Day:

  • North Carolina Driver's License or NC DMV State ID (unexpired or expired up to 1 year).
  • U.S. Passport or U.S. Passport Card (must be unexpired).
  • Out-of-State Driver's License (accepted only if you registered to vote in NC within 90 days of the election).
  • Free Voter Photo ID Card: Issued by Catawba County Board of Elections.
  • Photo ID Exception Form: If you lack an ID, complete an Exception Form at your assigned precinct and vote a ballot that counts.

[Campus Rule] Housing Roster Separation: Under NCSBE rules, campus housing rosters provided to the County Board of Elections are valid only if students are listed separately by residential dormitory facility.
