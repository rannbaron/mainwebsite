Student Photo ID Not On State Approved List

Student ID Card is NOT Accepted for Voting

Under North Carolina law, Robeson Community College student IDs are not approved for voting. You must present an approved alternative photo ID.

WHAT YOU CAN USE INSTEAD:

  • North Carolina Driver’s License or NC DMV State ID (unexpired or expired up to 1 year).
  • U.S. Passport or U.S. Passport Card (must be unexpired).
  • Out-of-State Driver’s License (accepted only if you registered to vote in NC within 90 days of the election).
  • Free Voter Photo ID Card: Issued by your county Board of Elections.
  • Photo ID Exception Form: If you lack an ID due to an allowable exception, you can complete an Exception Form at the check-in desk.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Robeson County, or if you moved to a new address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • [Photo ID] Photo ID Requirement: Present your approved physical Student ID (Not Approved by State Board), NC Driver's License, or US Passport.
  • [Roster] Housing Roster Separation Rule: Under NCSBE rules, an approved campus housing roster provided to the County Board of Elections serves as proof of residency only if it explicitly separates and identifies residents by individual campus housing facility / dormitory, and is presented alongside the student's photo ID.
Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • [Photo ID] Photo ID: Any approved photo identification.
  • [Proof] Proof of Residence: A document showing your name and current local Robeson County address (utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, lease, or university official letter; digital on smartphone is accepted).
Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.
