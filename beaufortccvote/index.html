Beaufort County Community College student early voting guide

Washington, NC | Beaufort County


Early voting hours

Early voting runs from Thursday, October 15 through Saturday, October 31, 2026. Any registered voter in Beaufort County can vote at any early voting site in the county.

Voter ID

Beaufort County Community College student IDs are approved for voting. You can present your physical BCCC Student ID.

Physical card required: Digital IDs on phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or photos) are not accepted under North Carolina law. You must present the physical plastic card.

Other acceptable photo IDs include: NC driver's license (unexpired or expired up to one year), U.S. passport, military or veteran ID, or a free voter photo ID card issued by your county board of elections.

Same-day registration

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you can register and vote on the same day during early voting. Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

To register during early voting, you must provide proof of your residential address in Beaufort County: an NC driver's license, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or government document showing your current address.
