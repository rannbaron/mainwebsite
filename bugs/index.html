📋 What Makes a Great Bug Report?

Our grading and automated course pipelines process hundreds of student submissions, lab reports, and quiz scores weekly. When something doesn't look right, submitting a detailed, reproducible bug report ensures the engineering team can diagnose and push a fix immediately.

1. Specific Assignment & Identifier

Always state the exact assignment name (e.g. Worksheet 10-1, Class Quiz 4 retake) and your student NetID or Duke email.

2. Expected vs. Actual Behavior

Explain clearly what you intended to happen vs. what our system did (e.g. "I scanned via Gradescope Mobile App, but received an automated email saying paper scan detected.").

3. Environment & Tools Used

Mention the device and software (e.g. "Gradescope App on iOS 18", "Apple Notes export", "Chrome on macOS", "Canvas gradebook sync").

4. Problem Location & Page

Mention the worksheet number, page, and problem number (e.g. "Worksheet 7-2, Page 2, Problem 3b") so we can open the exact LaTeX source.

💡 Fast Turnaround Guarantee: High-priority fixes (like exam reviews or current-week worksheets) are corrected in the underlying LaTeX repository and re-uploaded within 24 hours!
