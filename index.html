About Rann Bar-On

I specialize in early undergraduate mathematics education, having dedicated my career to it after completing my Ph.D. in Mathematics at Duke University in 2009 under the supervision of John Harer. For over a decade, I have taught and supervised Math 105L and 106L, entry-level laboratory calculus courses at Duke.

I have authored and edited laboratory curriculum for these courses, introducing technological and pedagogical innovations adapted to each cohort of students. I have served as a college (pre-major) advisor to dozens of undergraduate students and as major advisor to a few. In 2017, I won Duke’s Advisor of the Year Award, as chosen by the Academic Advising Center based on student feedback.

Outside the classroom, I conduct election data analysis, turnout modeling, and political geography research, developing interactive web tools to provide open, non-partisan data access to North Carolina voters, researchers, and students.
