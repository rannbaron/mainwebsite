Critical NC State Law Requirement

You MUST Bring a PHYSICAL Photo ID (No Phone/Mobile DukeCards!)

Under North Carolina voting law, voters must present an approved photo identification. Duke University's physical plastic DukeCard and Duke Student Voter ID Card are officially approved.

🚫 Mobile DukeCards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet are NOT ACCEPTED at polling sites under NC law.

✅ ACCEPTED: Physical plastic DukeCard, Duke Student Voter ID Card, NC Driver's License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID.

💡 Need a physical Duke voter card? If you only have a mobile DukeCard, you can get a free physical Duke Student Voter ID from the DukeCard Office (bring government-issued photo ID to pick it up).

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Durham County, or if you moved to a new local address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Physical DukeCard or Duke Student Voter ID Card (acts as both your Photo ID and residency verification).
  • ✓ Duke Housing provides an official on-campus student residential roster directly to Durham County election officials.
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Approved Photo ID: Physical DukeCard, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: A document showing your name and current Durham address (Duke official letter/portal page, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or lease; can be electronic on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address (in Durham, NC) or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Physical DukeCard, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport. Double-check you have the plastic card, not your phone!
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Physical DukeCard (on campus) or Durham address document (off campus).
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on dcovotes.com to preview candidates before you enter the booth.
  • 4. You Can Bring Notes: You are legally permitted to bring a written list or notes into the voting booth (just do not take photos or use your phone while voting).
