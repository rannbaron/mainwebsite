UNC Wilmington student early voting guide

Wilmington, NC | New Hanover County


Early voting hours

Early voting runs from Thursday, October 15 through Saturday, October 31, 2026. Any registered voter in New Hanover County can vote at any early voting site in the county.

Voter ID

UNC Wilmington student IDs are approved for voting. You can present your physical UNCW One Card.

Physical card required: Digital IDs on phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or photos) are not accepted under North Carolina law. You must present the physical plastic card.

Other acceptable photo IDs include: NC driver's license (unexpired or expired up to one year), U.S. passport, military or veteran ID, or a free voter photo ID card issued by your county board of elections.

Same-day registration

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you can register and vote on the same day during early voting. Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

To register during early voting, you must show proof of your residential address in New Hanover County.

On-campus students: UNC Wilmington provides an official student housing roster to the New Hanover County Board of Elections. You can verify your on-campus residential address with the campus roster paired with your student ID or any approved photo ID.

Off-campus students: You can show an NC driver's license, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or government document showing your name and current address in New Hanover County.
