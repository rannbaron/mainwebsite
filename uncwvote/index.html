UNC Wilmington Student ID is Valid for Voting!

The North Carolina State Board of Elections officially approved the Physical UNCW One Card for voter photo identification. You can use your physical plastic student ID to vote.

CRITICAL: PHYSICAL CARD REQUIRED. Digital IDs stored on mobile phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, photos) are NOT ACCEPTED under NC law.

Other Accepted Photo IDs: NC Driver’s License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID, or free county Board of Elections ID card.

Lacking an ID at the polls? Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at the check-in desk and cast a ballot that counts.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in New Hanover County, or if you moved to a new address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • [Photo ID] Photo ID Requirement: Present your approved physical Physical UNCW One Card, NC Driver's License, or US Passport.
  • [Roster] Housing Roster Separation Rule: Under NCSBE rules, an approved campus housing roster provided to the County Board of Elections serves as proof of residency only if it explicitly separates and identifies residents by individual campus housing facility / dormitory, and is presented alongside the student's photo ID.
Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • [Photo ID] Photo ID: Any approved photo identification.
  • [Proof] Proof of Residence: A document showing your name and current local New Hanover County address (utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, lease, or university official letter; digital on smartphone is accepted).
Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.
