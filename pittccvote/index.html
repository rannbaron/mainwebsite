Pitt Community College Student ID is Valid for Voting!

The North Carolina State Board of Elections officially approved the Physical PCC Student ID for voter photo identification. You can use your physical student ID to cast your ballot.

⚠️ CRITICAL: PHYSICAL CARD REQUIRED. Digital IDs stored on mobile phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, photos) are NOT ACCEPTED at polling sites under NC law.

✅ Other Accepted Photo IDs: NC Driver’s License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID, or free county BOE ID card.

💡 Need an ID on voting day? You can still vote! Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at the polling place or get a free voter photo ID at the Pitt County Board of Elections office.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Pitt County, or if you moved to a new address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Photo ID: An approved photo ID (or Physical PCC Student ID if approved, NC Driver's License, or US Passport).
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Campus housing verification (residential roster provided to election officials, or housing contract on student portal).
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Photo ID: Approved photo identification.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Document showing your name and current local Pitt County address (utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, lease, or university official letter; can be digital on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Bring a physical acceptable photo ID. Remember: digital phone images are not permitted.
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Proof of your current address in Pitt County.
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on the Pitt County Board Of Elections portal.
  • 4. Notes Allowed: You can bring a printed voter guide or written notes into the voting booth.
