Mandatory NC State Law Requirement

You MUST Bring a PHYSICAL Photo ID (No Phone/Digital Cards!)

PHOTO ID MANDATORY: Under North Carolina law, all voters must show an approved photo ID. The NCCU Eagle Card is officially approved by the NC State Board of Elections. You must present your physical plastic ID card (phone/mobile images are not accepted). NC Driver's License and US Passport are also accepted.

🚫 Mobile digital IDs on your phone are NOT ACCEPTED at polling sites under North Carolina law.

✅ ACCEPTED: Physical NCCU Eagle Card, NC Driver's License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID.

💡 Need an ID on the spot? You can still vote! Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at the polling place or obtain a free voter photo ID from the Durham County Board of Elections.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Durham County, or if you moved to a new local address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Physical NCCU Eagle Card (physical card).
  • ✓ NCCU Residential Life provides residential verification to the Durham County Board of Elections. Your physical Eagle Card acts as valid photo ID and proof of enrollment.
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Approved Photo ID: Physical NCCU Eagle Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Document showing your name and current local Durham County address (university official portal/letter, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or lease; can be electronic on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Physical NCCU Eagle Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport. Double-check you have the plastic card, not your phone!
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Physical campus ID (on campus) or local address document (off campus).
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on Durham County Board of Elections to preview candidates before you vote.
  • 4. You Can Bring Notes: You are legally permitted to bring a written list or notes into the voting booth (just do not take photos while voting).
