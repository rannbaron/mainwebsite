Surry Community College student early voting guide

Dobson, NC | Surry County


Early voting hours

Early voting runs from Thursday, October 15 through Saturday, October 31, 2026. Any registered voter in Surry County can vote at any early voting site in the county.

Voter ID

Photo ID required: Surry Community College student IDs are not approved for voting under North Carolina law. You must present an approved alternative photo ID, such as an NC driver's license, U.S. passport, or free county voter photo ID card.

Acceptable photo IDs include: NC driver's license (unexpired or expired up to one year), U.S. passport, military or veteran ID, or a free voter photo ID card issued by Surry County Board of Elections.

Same-day registration

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you can register and vote on the same day during early voting. Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

To register during early voting, you must provide proof of your residential address in Surry County: an NC driver's license, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or government document showing your current address.
