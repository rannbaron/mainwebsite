Student ID Card is NOT Accepted for Voting

Under North Carolina law, student IDs cannot be used for voting unless the institution applied for and received approval from the State Board of Elections. Richmond Community College student IDs are not approved for voting purposes.

✅ WHAT YOU CAN USE INSTEAD:

  • North Carolina Driver’s License or NC DMV State ID (can be expired up to 1 year, or unexpired).
  • U.S. Passport or U.S. Passport Card (must be unexpired).
  • Out-of-State Driver’s License (accepted only if you registered to vote in NC within 90 days of the election).
  • Free Voter Photo ID Card: Issued on the spot for free at the Richmond County Board of Elections office!
  • Photo ID Exception Form: If you lack an approved ID due to a reasonable impediment, you can complete an Exception Form at the polling site and cast a ballot that counts.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Richmond County, or if you moved to a new address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Photo ID: An approved photo ID (or Student ID (Not Approved by State Board) if approved, NC Driver's License, or US Passport).
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Campus housing verification (residential roster provided to election officials, or housing contract on student portal).
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Photo ID: Approved photo identification.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Document showing your name and current local Richmond County address (utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, lease, or university official letter; can be digital on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Bring a physical acceptable photo ID. Remember: digital phone images are not permitted.
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Proof of your current address in Richmond County.
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on the Richmond County Board Of Elections portal.
  • 4. Notes Allowed: You can bring a printed voter guide or written notes into the voting booth.
