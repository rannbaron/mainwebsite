Voter ID

Photo ID required: Montreat College student IDs are not approved for voting under North Carolina law. You must present an approved alternative photo ID, such as an NC driver's license, U.S. passport, or free county voter photo ID card.

Acceptable photo IDs include: NC driver's license (unexpired or expired up to one year), U.S. passport, military or veteran ID, or a free voter photo ID card issued by Buncombe County Board of Elections.

Same-day registration

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you can register and vote on the same day during early voting. Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

To register during early voting, you must show proof of your residential address in Buncombe County.

On-campus students: Montreat College provides a student housing roster to the Buncombe County Board of Elections. Under NCSBE Numbered Memo 2023-05, because this is a private institution, students relying on the campus housing roster as proof of residence must present their physical Student ID (Not Approved by State Board). An out-of-state driver's license or passport cannot be paired with a private-school housing roster. Under state rules, the housing roster is only valid if it lists residents separated by individual residential facility or dormitory.

Off-campus students: You can show an NC driver's license, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or government document showing your name and current address in Buncombe County.
