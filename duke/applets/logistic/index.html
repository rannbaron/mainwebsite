Logistic Model with Harvesting

  • The Equation: Models a population that grows logistically but has a constant amount (H) removed per time step.
  • Multiple Starts: Check the boxes at the top to spawn up to 3 different populations. Drag their starting points up and down the Time Series axis!
  • Phase Plot (Top Right): Shows the growth rate against current population. Where the parabola crosses the x-axis, the population is in Equilibrium. Grab the Harvest slider to slide the parabola down!
  • Time Series (Left): Shows how the population changes over time based on your Initial Populations. The equilibria act as horizontal asymptotes.
  • Extinction: If you increase Harvesting (H) too much, the parabola drops entirely below the x-axis, the asymptotes annihilate, and extinction is inevitable!
