How to Use the Slope Field Explorer

Navigation & Viewport

  • Pan: Click and drag anywhere on the blank graph background to move around.
  • Zoom: Use your mouse scroll wheel (desktop) or pinch-to-zoom (mobile) to zoom in and out.
  • Manual Limits: Type exact numbers into the X and Y limit boxes at the top to jump to a specific view. Click Reset View to return to the default auto-scaling.

Entering Equations

  • Type your differential equation into the Eq boxes at the top. The slope field will update immediately.
  • Implicit Math is Supported! You do not need to type multiplication stars. Writing xy, 2x, or x(y+1) will be automatically understood.

Interacting with Solutions

  • Drag and Drop: Click and drag any of the colored dots on the graph to change the Initial Value condition.
  • Manual Entry: Double-click (or double-tap) any colored dot to type exact coordinates.
