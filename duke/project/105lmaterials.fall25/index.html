Gradescope Submissions, Returns, and Resubmissions

All work for 105L will be submitted on Gradescope. Here are some guidelines, links, and instructions for submission:

  • Once you have completed your work, you need to scan it to a PDF if it’s done on paper. You should use a document scanner app on your phone. Ideally, you should use the Gradescope Mobile App to submit. You can install that from your phone or tablet’s app store. Instructions for using the app can be found here. You may choose to use a different document scanner app, as as Adobe Scan. However, assignments submitted without a document scanner app are not acceptable. Specifically, you may not simply use your phone’s camera app.

  • When logging into Gradescope, be sure to use the ‘School Credentials’ button:

    (You can instead log into Gradescope via Canvas if you wish! That will log you in automatically.)

  • After you’ve logged in, find the correct assignment in the Assignments area, and follow the instructions here.

  • Each question will be scored 1 (perfect), 0.9 (minor error), 0.5 (partly correct), or 0. Each question will get commented upon. You will then use comments to correct your work and resubmit.

  • Each assignment will have a separate resubmit Gradescope assignment:

  • After you resubmit, graders will regrade each question you ask them to regrade by using the ‘Regrade’ checkbox below each question number. Once resubmissions are returned, you will get a ‘pass’ or ’no-pass’ on each assignment. You will get an email from me if you did not pass an assignment. Pass/no-passes will also be recorded in the Gradebook on Canvas.

Getting Help

You will need help with this class! Everyone will. Math at Duke is challenging, and this class will ask a lot of you, regardless of your math background. There are many resources available for help. Here’s an outline with links:

  • Help Room: Located in the Classroom building (Room 137), the help room is open around 25 hours a week from 5-10pm Su-Th. It is staffed by TAs, graders, and others. You can go at any time to get help with your work. You can go do homework there and get help when you need it. It’s a great space to have quiet work time, but have help readily available.
  • Office Hours: I will have two hours of in person office hours, and one hour of virtual office hours per week (see hours above). Here’s a Zoom link for the virtual office hours.
  • Individual meetings: I’m available to meet one on one in person or by Zoom as needed. Drop me an email! I’m also available for quicker questions before/after class.
  • The Academic Resource Center: Duke’s ARC provides multiple layers of support for classes. From peer tutoring to study groups, this is one of the most useful resources Duke provides. Make an appointment with an advisor there, or sign up for a study group!
