\paragraph*{Exercises from Strang} P. 314 2,4,11,15,16,18,29,31,34; P.436 1,5-11,15,19,20.

\paragraph*{Additional Question} Consider the matrix $$A=\left(\begin{array}{rrrr}
9 & -1 & -2 & 4 \\
-4 & 2 & 1 & -2 \\
8 & 0 & 0 & 4 \\
-10 & 2 & 3 & -4
\end{array}\right)$$

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Find the eigenvalues of $A$. (Hint: You need to find $\chi_A(t)=|tI-A|$, the characteristic polynomial of $A$. Remember that you can do both row and column operations before computing that determinant! If you're careful, one row and one column operation results in a matrix that is \emph{almost} upper triangular.)
  \item Find eigenvectors corresponding to each of your eigenvalues.
  \item What are the algebraic and geometric multiplicities of each of your eigenvalues?
  \item Is $A$ diagonalizable? Explain your answer.
\end{enumerate}
