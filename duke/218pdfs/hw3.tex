\section*{Extra Problems}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Given the three points $$P = (0,2,-1), Q=(2,1,-8), R=(1,3,0)$$ find the parameters $a$, $b$, and $c$ in the equation of the plane $z=ax+by+c$ so that the plane contains the three points. Use the following steps:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Write down the system of equations satisfied by this data;
    \item Write the equations in matrix form;
    \item Use Gaussian Elimination (any of the three methods) or LU decomposition to solve the problem;
    \item Write the equation of the plane.
  \end{enumerate}
  \item Consider the matrix $$A=\begin{pmatrix}[cccc] \llap{-}3 & 13 & 38 \\ 3 & \llap{-}2  & 4  \\ 2 & \llap{-}7 & \llap{-}19 \end{pmatrix}.$$  Using only row-additions (no row swaps or row scaling), find elementary matrices $E_1,E_2,\ldots,E_k$ such that $E_k\cdots E_2E_1A=U$ is upper triangular. Also write down the LU decomposition of $A$.
\end{enumerate}
