+++ Back to Main 105L Page

Note that this is the old Spring 2020 site. Links below may be broken or outdated.

Homework should be completed following the class on that day, before the next class. For example, Day 1-2 (01/08/20) homework should be completed before Day 1-3 (01/10/20). It is suggested that homework be done the day it is covered in class.

Exception: Review day homeworks should be completed before that review day.

Notation like ‘1.5/2, 4, 6’ refers to chapter 1.5 of the textbook, questions 2, 4, and 6. ‘Chapter 3 Review’ refers to the review questions at the end of Chapter 3 of the textbook.

All homework from the textbook has solutions that can be found on Sakai under ‘Resources’ -> ‘Textbook Solutions’.
