Voter ID

UNC Greensboro student IDs are approved for voting. You can present your physical SpartanCard.

Physical card required: Digital IDs on phones (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or photos) are not accepted under North Carolina law. You must present the physical plastic card.

Other acceptable photo IDs include: NC driver's license (unexpired or expired up to one year), U.S. passport, military or veteran ID, or a free voter photo ID card issued by your county board of elections.

Same-day registration

If you missed the voter registration deadline, you can register and vote on the same day during early voting. Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

To register during early voting, you must show proof of your residential address in Guilford County.

On-campus students: UNC Greensboro provides an official student housing roster to the Guilford County Board of Elections. You can verify your on-campus residential address with the campus roster paired with your student ID or any approved photo ID.

Off-campus students: You can show an NC driver's license, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or government document showing your name and current address in Guilford County.
