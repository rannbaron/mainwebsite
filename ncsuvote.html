Mandatory NC State Law Requirement

You MUST Bring a PHYSICAL Photo ID (No Phone/Digital Cards!)

PHYSICAL PHOTO ID REQUIRED: Under NC voting law, all voters must show an acceptable photo ID. The NC State Wolfpack One Card is officially approved by the State Board of Elections. You must present your physical plastic card (mobile/phone IDs are not accepted under NC law). NC Driver's License, state ID, or US Passport are also accepted.

🚫 Mobile digital IDs on your phone are NOT ACCEPTED at polling sites under North Carolina law.

✅ ACCEPTED: Physical Wolfpack One Card, NC Driver's License, US Passport, Military/Veteran ID.

💡 Need an ID on the spot? You can still vote! Fill out a Photo ID Exception Form at the polling place or obtain a free voter photo ID from the Wake County Board of Elections.

Missed the Voter Registration Deadline?

Same-Day Voter Registration is Available!

In North Carolina, if you are not yet registered to vote in Wake County, or if you moved to a new local address, you can register and vote on the same visit exclusively during Early Voting. (Same-day registration is not allowed on Election Day).

🏠 On-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Physical Wolfpack One Card (physical card).
  • ✓ On-campus NC State students can use their physical Wolfpack One Card along with NC State housing verification (accessible via MyPack Portal or housing agreement).
🏢 Off-Campus Residents

What to Bring:

  • ✓ Approved Photo ID: Physical Wolfpack One Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport.
  • ✓ Proof of Residence: Document showing your name and current local Wake County address (university official portal/letter, utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, or lease; can be electronic on your phone).
⚖️ Student Voting Rights: College students have the established legal right to choose whether to register and vote at their college address or their permanent home address. You must choose one and vote only once per election.

📋 Pre-Voting Checklist

  • 1. Physical Photo ID: Physical Wolfpack One Card, NC Driver’s License, or US Passport. Double-check you have the plastic card, not your phone!
  • 2. Proof of Residence (If Registering): Physical campus ID (on campus) or local address document (off campus).
  • 3. Check Sample Ballot: Look up your sample ballot on Wake County Board of Elections to preview candidates before you vote.
  • 4. You Can Bring Notes: You are legally permitted to bring a written list or notes into the voting booth (just do not take photos while voting).
